FAQ

1. Which cameras are supported?
Two kinds, detected automatically. Cameras from 2020 on (a6700, a7 IV, a7R V, a7C II, ZV-E10 II, a1, FX30…) send full RAW, JPEG, HEIF and video; tested with the Sony a6700, more models during the beta. Cameras before 2020 (a6000–a6600, a7 II/III, a7R III/IV, RX100 III–VII, ZV-1…) send JPEG and video — they cannot send RAW over Wi-Fi; on the camera use Send to Smartphone → Select on Smartphone.
2. How do I prepare the camera?
Cameras from 2020 on: open MENU → Network → Cnct./PC Remote → PC Remote Function and set PC Remote to On. Then open Wi-Fi Direct Info and press Connect with QR Code at the bottom (the trash button) — a QR code appears on the camera screen. The same screen also shows the network name and password, if you prefer to type them. Scan it in CamDrop. Don't use Smartphone Connection: on these cameras its QR code installs Sony's app, it is not a Wi-Fi network. Cameras before 2020: press Playback, then MENU → Send to Smartphone → Select on Smartphone.
3. The app says "The camera is not in transfer mode".
The camera has left the transfer screen. Open the Wi-Fi screen on the camera again (Wi-Fi Direct Info with PC Remote on; on cameras before 2020, Send to Smartphone) and try once more.
4. The iPhone doesn't join the camera's Wi-Fi.
Make sure you allow the "join network" prompt from iOS. If it still fails: forget the camera network in iOS Settings → Wi-Fi, then scan the QR code again. Restarting the camera's Wi-Fi (turn PC Remote off and on; on cameras before 2020, leave and reopen Send to Smartphone) also helps.
5. The app can't find the camera — "Local Network" permission.
CamDrop needs the Local Network permission to reach the camera. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → CamDrop and turn it on.
6. Where are my RAW files?
In Photos, a RAW+JPEG pair is saved as one photo: the JPEG is what you see, and the RAW (ARW) is attached to it — editors like Lightroom will pick it up automatically. Every transferred file is also available in the Files app under On My iPhone → CamDrop.
7. How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings in CamDrop → Restore Purchases. Family Sharing is supported.
8. How do I send a connection log?
If something doesn't work, a log helps a lot. In CamDrop: Settings → Support → Export connection log, then email it to wertyal094@gmail.com. The log contains only the camera model and file names — no photos, no passwords.
